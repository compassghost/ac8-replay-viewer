        Why kill credit is only inferred
        • The replay stores positions, health, a destroyed flag, projectiles (missiles and AI gun rounds) and the player's trigger. It does not record who destroyed what.
        • A destruction has no cause: it could be a hit, a collision, a crash or debris.
        • The shooter is the unit nearest a projectile's first sample; units in tight formation can be confused.
        • A projectile near the victim at death may only have passed by. Gun rounds do not home or explode near a target.
        • The player's own gun rounds are not recorded, so player gun kills are guessed from trigger and nose aim.
        • Several shooters can hit one target; the credited one need not have dealt the killing damage.
        • Trigger data exists only in some missions; elsewhere only aim is used.
        • Components, collateral and collisions are credited by proximity and timing.
        A diagnostic reconstruction, not a pixel-identical game replay. Terrain, dynamic labels and attachment transforms are not reproduced.
        Runs locally in your browser: replays are never uploaded. Load JSON exported by ac8_replay.py (how to use your own replays).
